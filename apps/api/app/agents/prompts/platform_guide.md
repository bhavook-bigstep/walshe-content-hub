## PLATFORM OVERVIEW

Walsh Content Hub is a business-to-business destination content and marketing platform from The Walshe Group. It is for the travel trade, not for consumers.

- Content Providers (tourism boards and destination organizations) publish a verified catalog of destination content: events, places, opportunities, offers and itineraries, with cover photos, text and media.
- Tourism Agents (travel and tourism agents) search that catalog, save what they need into collections, and turn it into marketing assets in the Design Studio (social images, stories, multi-page pamphlets and short videos). They can personalise designs with their own branding, export them, and schedule or publish them to a simulated social layer that tracks engagement.
- Platform administrators manage accounts and verify content providers.

How content flows: a provider creates an entry in their catalog. New entries start as Draft, and no agent can see a draft. The provider publishes an entry by setting it to Public (every agent) or Private (only agents the provider has invited). Agents find published entries in their Catalog, save them to a Collection, start a Design Studio project from that collection, build and save a design, then schedule or publish it from Social. The provider's Performance page shows how often agents use each entry.

Getting an account
- On Register, choose "I'm a travel agent" (email + password of 8+ characters; signed in straight away) or "I'm a tourism board / content provider".
- Providers use "Request access", which asks for Organization, Your name, Markets, Email, Password and Confirm password. They then see an "Application under review" holding screen until a platform administrator approves the organization. Approval gives full access. The holding screen has "Contact the team" and "Sign out" buttons.
- Sign in with email and password; expired sessions sign you out automatically.

Common workspace features (both roles)
- Left sidebar navigation (collapsible to an icon rail on desktop; a menu button on mobile). The top bar has breadcrumbs and a light/dark theme toggle (defaults to the device setting; your choice is remembered).
- Profile & settings is at the bottom of the sidebar, next to Sign out. There you can set your Display name, Avatar colour and Bio, and turn on Reduce motion.
- Assistant: the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner opens this chat. It can explain how any part of the platform works, find content and suggest what to post, and it remembers the conversation while the page stays open (refreshing the page starts a new chat). Suggested prompts include "Find events in Galway", "What should I post this week?" and "How do collections work?". Agents only get content they are allowed to see; providers get their own catalog, drafts included. When content matches, it is listed as cards under the answer, with a link to start a design (agents) or to go to the catalog (providers).

## CONCEPTS

- Catalog: the library of destination content. Each provider has a single catalog that holds all of their entries. The agent's Catalog page is a searchable view of every entry the agent is allowed to see, across providers.
- Entry: one piece of destination content. Every entry has a type, a title, a location (country, state/region, city), an optional season, an optional cover photo, an expiry setting, type-specific details, and items.
- Entry types and their detail fields:
  - Event: Start date, End date, Venue, Ticket link, Expected attendance
  - Place: Region, Latitude, Longitude, Accessibility notes
  - Opportunity: a trade opportunity, with Deadline, Commission / rate, Partner, Terms link
  - Offer: Price from, Currency, Valid until, Booking link
  - Itinerary: Duration (days), Stops (comma-separated), Difficulty, Map link
- Items: the content inside an entry. An item is either a text block (optional title plus text) or a media file (an image or a video). Agents use items in the Design Studio, and they appear in the entry's detail view.
- Cover photo: the entry's card image in both catalogs. It can be uploaded or generated by AI from a prompt.
- Season: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter or Year-round.
- Visibility (set per entry):
  - Draft: hidden from all agents. This is the default.
  - Public: visible to every agent.
  - Private: visible only to agents the provider has invited.
  - Moving an entry back to Draft unpublishes it.
- Invited agents: the list of agent accounts a provider has invited by email. Invited agents can see that provider's Private entries.
- Brand-safe: a flag a provider can set on an entry ("Mark as brand-safe — Verified for the trade"). Agents see a "Verified" badge on catalog cards.
- Expiry and display statuses:
  - An entry either never expires or expires on a set date. For events, the expiry can be set to the event's end date in one click.
  - Agent catalog badges: "Available", "Expiring soon" (close to its expiry date) and "Expired".
  - Expired entries still appear, greyed out, but agents cannot save or use them. They are dropped from collections and blocked when sending.
- Collection: an agent's named group of saved catalog entries. A collection holds references to entries, not copies, so it always shows the current version of each one. Entries that are no longer available drop out automatically.
- Project: a saved Design Studio composition. It contains the design (its scenes) and the media attached to it: collections, uploads and AI-generated images. In Social, a project is called a "Composition".
- Template: a preset starting point for a design in a given format.
- Format:
  - Social image: 1080×1080
  - Story: 1080×1920
  - Pamphlet: 1240×1754, with 4 pages
- Scene: one artboard or page in the Design Studio. Multiple scenes form a storyboard. Each scene has a duration and a transition, which are used when generating a video.
- Brand kit: the agent's saved logo, colours and contact details. It pre-fills personalisation in the studio.
- Pre-send check: an automatic check that runs before a post is scheduled or published. It blocks the send and explains how to fix any problems.
- Organization: the provider's organization profile (logo, name, blurb, markets), shown with a verification status of either "Verified board" or "Pending verification".

## TOURISM AGENT GUIDE

The agent sidebar has these sections, in this order: Overview, Catalog, Collections, Templates, Design Studio, Projects, Brand kit, Social, Engagement.

Typical workflow
1. Catalog: find content and use Save to collection.
2. Collections: open the collection and choose Open in Design Studio.
3. Design Studio: place media, use the AI Builder, personalise, then Save project.
4. Export the design (PNG, PDF, email HTML or MP4 video) and/or go to Social to schedule or publish it.
5. Check results in Engagement.

### Overview ("Agent home")
- Stat tiles (Approved content, Impressions, Clicks, Average CTR); "Worth sending next" suggestions of unused, timely entries with a reason; an "Engagement by post" chart and Top posts; "Recent approved content"; quick links to Catalog, Design Studio, Social and Engagement.

### Catalog
- Search by keyword and filter by Country, State / region, City (these cascade), Season and Type (Event, Place, Opportunity, Offer, Itinerary). Then click Search. Clear resets the filters. A result count is shown.
- Each card shows the cover image, a Verified badge, the type and destination, a status badge (Available / Expiring soon / Expired), the expiry date if there is one, the title, a short description and the number of items.
- Click a card's image or title to open the entry detail view. It shows everything about the entry: location, season, expiry, markets, type-specific details, highlights, any extra sections, text copy, an image gallery, videos, and which provider and organization it came from.
- Save to collection:
  1. Click "Save to collection" on a card.
  2. In the dialog, either click an existing collection, or type a "New collection name" and click "Create & save".
  3. A confirmation appears, and the button changes to "Saved · in N" to show how many of your collections contain the entry.
  4. Collections that already hold the entry are marked "Saved ✓".
  5. Expired entries show "Expired — can't save".
- What you see: only Public entries and the Private entries of providers who invited you. You never see drafts.

### Collections
- Create an empty collection: type a name under "New collection" and click Create. You can also create collections from the Catalog's save dialog.
- Each collection card shows thumbnails and the item count. If saved items are no longer available, the card says "N no longer available".
- Click a collection to open it. In the collection view you can:
  - rename it by editing the Name field (the change saves when you leave the field)
  - open any entry's detail view by clicking it
  - remove an entry with ✕
  - delete the whole collection with "Delete collection"
  - click "Open in Design Studio"
- Open in Design Studio creates a new project named after the collection and opens it in the studio with the collection's media loaded. The button is disabled when the collection is empty.

### Templates
- Template cards show the template's format, name and description. Hover over (or tab to) a card to reveal two buttons:
  - Preview opens a dialog with the format outline and description, and a "Use template" button.
  - Use opens the Design Studio in that template's format.
- Templates are a format starting point. To keep your work, click "Save to projects". To add catalog media afterwards, use the media drawer's + menu and choose "Add a collection".

### Design Studio
The studio is a full-screen, pannable canvas with floating panels.

Starting a project
- The best way to start is from a collection (Collections → Open in Design Studio), or by reopening a saved project from Projects.
- If you open the Design Studio from the sidebar with no project, a banner prompts you to "Start a project from a collection to use its media".

Top bar
- Shows the project name being edited.
- Storyboard button (with the scene count): opens the scene panel.
- Add media: upload an image or generate one with AI.
- Generate video: renders an MP4 of your scenes.
- Save project: shows as "Save to projects" when the design hasn't been saved yet. This creates the project in Projects. Save after making canvas changes so they are kept.

Media drawer (left; open it with the semicircle handle)
- Titled "Media library". It groups media by each attached collection, then "Uploads", then "AI generated".
- Each collection entry gives you a ready-made entry card image plus the entry's individual images.
- Place media: click a tile to place it on the active scene, or drag it onto the canvas to drop it at that spot.
- The + button offers:
  - Add a collection: attach another of your collections to this project.
  - Upload an image: PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or AVIF.
  - Generate with AI: type a prompt (for example "sunset over the cliffs") and click Generate.
- Uploaded and generated images are saved to the project. If you add media with no project open, an "Untitled project" is created for it.

Canvas controls
- Drag empty space to pan. Use the mouse wheel or trackpad to zoom toward the cursor. The bottom-right controls offer −, Fit and +.
- Click an element to select it. Drag to move it, use the handles to resize it, and press Delete or Backspace to remove it.
- Click a scene's artboard to make it the active scene. New content always lands on the active scene.

Right tool rail (each tool opens a panel)
- Add elements:
  - Text: type your text, then click "Add text".
  - Shapes: Add rect, Add ellipse, Add line.
  - Background: pick a colour, then click "Set background".
  - Images: "Add image: …" buttons for the project's media.
- Format & size: choose Social image, Story or Pamphlet. Changing the format starts a fresh canvas in the new size, so pick the format before you design.
- Personalise:
  - Upload a logo and fill in Name, Email, Phone and Website, plus a Custom offer.
  - Click "Apply to page" to add them to the current scene.
  - The fields are pre-filled from your Brand kit (logo, contact name, email, website) when available.
- Export / download:
  - Export PNG downloads the current scene as a full-resolution image.
  - Export PDF downloads all scenes as pages, which is the natural choice for pamphlets.
  - Export email HTML downloads an email-ready HTML file.

Storyboard panel (scenes)
- Click "+ Add scene" to add a scene.
- Select a scene, rename it, reorder it with the up/down arrows, or remove it.
- Set each scene's Duration (0.5–15 seconds) and its "Transition to next scene": Cut (none), Crossfade, Slide left or Zoom.
- Scenes appear on the canvas as a chain of artboards linked by arrows.

Generate video
- Turns the ordered scenes into an MP4 and downloads it automatically.
- Each scene plays for its set duration, captioned with the scene's text (or the entry title) and its catalog image, with narration. Scenes are joined using the transitions you chose.
- The video uses only approved catalog imagery. If rendering isn't available, the studio shows "Video rendering unavailable."

AI Studio (the "AI Studio" button at bottom centre) has two tabs:
- AI Builder:
  1. Write a "Design brief" describing what you want, for example the audience, the message and the tone.
  2. Click "Generate design".
  3. The Builder uses the catalog entries in the project's collections (shown as "N catalog items selected") to place their approved images and write copy onto the active scene.
  4. Then refine the design manually.
  - The Builder only works with your project's collection content. Without it, the panel says no catalog items are available. It does not invent images.
- Planner:
  - Click "Generate creative plan" to get a headline, body and call to action drafted from your selected content.
  - The plan shows a status of "Grounded ✓" or "Needs review". It lists any issues, and under "Grounded in" it shows the source fields that each claim comes from.
  - Use the plan as copy guidance for your design.

### Projects
- Lists your saved compositions, each with its format and item count.
- Open in studio reopens a project. Delete removes it.

### Brand kit
- Fields: Logo URL, Primary colour, Accent colour, Contact name, Contact email, Website. Click Save brand kit to store them.
- These details pre-fill the studio's Personalise panel, so you don't have to retype them.

### Social
Social schedules or publishes a saved project to a social channel. The social connection is simulated: nothing is actually posted to real Facebook, Instagram, X or LinkedIn accounts.

Steps
1. Choose a Composition (one of your saved projects).
2. Choose a Channel: facebook, instagram, x or linkedin.
3. Optionally set Schedule at (date and time).
4. Click Schedule, or click Publish now to publish immediately.
5. You can click Run pre-send check first to see whether the post is ready.

Pre-send check
- Every schedule or publish runs the pre-send check first.
- If the check passes, you see "All checks passed — this post is ready to send."
- If it fails, the send is blocked and a "Fix before sending" list explains each problem and how to fix it. Common problems:
  - The post has no catalog content yet. Fix: add approved catalog content to the project.
  - Items are no longer available (expired, withdrawn, or outside your permissions). Fix: remove them or replace them with current content.
  - Items changed since you added them.
  - The channel isn't supported.

Posts list
- Shows the posts you sent during the current visit, with channel, scheduled or published time, and a status of "scheduled" or "published".
- A post that has already been published can't be published again.

### Engagement
- Totals: Impressions, Clicks, Engagement and Average CTR.
- An "Engagement by post" chart and a per-post table with columns Post, Impressions, Clicks, Engagement and CTR.
- Because publishing is simulated, these figures are illustrative. They are not live metrics from real social networks.

## CONTENT PROVIDER GUIDE

The provider sidebar has these sections: Overview, Catalog, Performance, Organization. A new provider account can't use the workspace until a platform administrator approves the organization. Until then, it sees the "Application under review" screen.

### Overview ("Provider home")
- Stat tiles (Catalog entries, Content items, Uploaded assets) and a preview of your latest entries with links to open them.

### Catalog
- The catalog page groups your entries into three sections:
  - Public: visible to every agent
  - Private: visible only to invited agents
  - Drafts: hidden from agents until you publish them
- Each card shows the cover, a visibility badge, the title, the type and destination, and the item count. Expired entries are greyed out and marked "Expired".
- Click a card to open its entry page.

Create an entry: click New entry. The form contains:
- Type: Event, Place, Opportunity, Offer or Itinerary.
- Title: required.
- Visibility: "Draft — hidden from agents" (the default), "Public — every agent", or "Private — invited agents only". Choosing Public or Private publishes the entry.
- Location & season (a collapsible section): Country (required), State / region and City (these cascade), and Season. The destination label is built automatically from the location.
- [Type] details (collapsible): the type-specific fields listed in Concepts, such as an event's Start date, End date, Venue and Ticket link.
- Cover photo (collapsible, optional), with three choices:
  - No cover
  - Upload: PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or AVIF
  - AI generate: enter an image prompt, or leave it blank to use the title and destination
- Expiry (collapsible): leave "Never expires (lives forever)" ticked, or untick it and pick an Expiry date. For events with an End date, "Use event end date" fills the expiry in one click.
- Click Create entry.

After creating the entry, open it to add text and media items. Items are what agents see and pull into their designs.

### Entry page
- Header buttons: Delete, Edit and Add item.
- Provenance line: "Created by [email] · [organization]", plus an Expired badge if the entry has expired.
- Cover photo: shows the current cover. You can upload a new one ("Upload a new cover"), or type a prompt under "Or generate one" and click AI generate.
- Details: the type-specific fields you filled in.
- Items: a list of text and media items. Remove any item with ✕.
- Add item:
  1. Choose the Text tab (optional title plus text) or the Media tab (optional title plus an image or video file).
  2. Click Add item.
- Edit: opens the same form used for creation. Change the type, title, visibility, location, season, details or expiry, then click Save changes. The cover is managed in the Cover photo section instead.
- Delete: asks you to confirm, then removes the entry and returns you to the catalog.
- Review section:
  - Visibility: move the entry between Draft, Public and Private, then click Save. Setting an entry back to Draft unpublishes it, and agents no longer see it.
  - Mark as brand-safe ("Verified for the trade"): tick or untick it, then click Save.
  - Reason to send back and Send back for changes: record a reason for returning the entry for changes. The reason then appears on the entry under "Sent back for changes". This is a review note. To hide the entry from agents, set its Visibility to Draft.

Expiry behaviour for agents
- As an entry nears its expiry date, agents see an "Expiring soon" badge.
- After the expiry date, agents still see the entry greyed out, but they can't save it or use it in sends.
- To keep content usable, update the expiry date or choose Never expires.

### Performance
- Shows how agents use your content.
- Total uses: the number of agent compositions that include your entries.
- Total reach: impressions from posts made with those compositions.
- A table lists each piece of content with its Uses and Reach, highest first.

### Organization
- Profile:
  - Shows your verification status: "Verified board" or "Pending verification".
  - Upload a logo (jpg, jpeg or png only; SVG is not accepted).
  - Edit Organization name, Blurb and Markets (comma-separated, for example "Australia, New Zealand, Fiji").
  - Click Save changes.
- Invited agents: controls who can see your Private entries.
  - Enter an agent's email and click Invite. The email must belong to an existing registered tourism agent account. Otherwise you'll see "No tourism agent found with that email address".
  - Invited agents appear as chips. Click ✕ to remove one, which also removes their access to your Private entries.
- Team: colleagues who manage your organization's content, each shown as Active or Pending.
  - Under "Invite a colleague", enter their Email, optionally their Name, and a Temporary password (at least 8 characters).
  - Click Invite colleague.
  - The colleague joins your organization and can sign in with that email and temporary password.
